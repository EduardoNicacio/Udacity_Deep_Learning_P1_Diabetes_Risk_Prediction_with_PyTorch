## Suggestions to Make Your Project Stand Out

- **Implement Suggested Optimizations**: Implement one or more of the additional improvements you proposed in Section 6.5 (Part B), such as Early Stopping, Weight Decay (L2 Regularization), or a Learning Rate Scheduler, and track the results in your comparison table.
- **Handle Imbalance**: Find the original, imbalanced version of the CDC dataset (with ~14% diabetes prevalence) and retrain your best model on it. Then tune and/or regularize the model for the specific challenge using techniques such as class weighting (e.g., `pos_weight` in your loss function) or oversampling (e.g., SMOTE).
- **Feature Engineering**: Implement basic feature engineering before the model. Create new features (e.g., interaction terms such as BMI *Age or HighBP* HighChol), remove correlated features, and keep only the most informative features. Analyze how this tailored "domain knowledge" improves performance relative to hyperparameter tuning alone.
